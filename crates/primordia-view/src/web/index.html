How to read this
Each bright colour is one colony: a connected group of related cells (copies of each other)
Slate = loners, not part of a sizeable colony
Taller = more stored energy
Faded = dormant (out of energy)
Low green ground = food (free energy)
Orange plumes = hydrothermal vents
